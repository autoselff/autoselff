About

A survival game set on JSP32, humanity's third discovered planet with confirmed life. You play as a scientist whose transport crashes on ANTA3, a polar continent resembling Earth's Antarctica but with denser flora and fauna adapted to extreme cold. Stranded near the destroyed research station RS NO12, you must reach RS NO13 through uncharted territory. The planet's thin atmosphere and strong magnetic field allow frequent meteorite impacts, providing surface-accessible minerals.
