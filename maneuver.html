About

The goal of the game is to maintain the starting position using rcs thrusters.

LORE

A spaceship traveling back from the Moon was struck by a meteoroid. Thankfully the crew is OK. However the collision disabled several systems in the avionics bay, including the inertial navigation subsystem, and partially damaged the propulsion system. Help the pilot execute the final MANEUVER and put the ship on a trajectory towards the safety of low earth orbit. Be careful though, as now that only one engine is functional, the ship won't give you an easy time.

GAMEPLAY

Despite the damage, your ship's backup computer is still running and about to COUNT DOWN for how many seconds you need to burn the engine. Use WSAD and EQ to control the orientation in space in order to keep the ship facing in the right direction and SPACE to start and shutdown the engine. No digital or mechanical attitude aid is available so your only reference point is the mother earth in the window, better no lose sight of her. Pay attention to what the Mission Control is radioing to you and complete the burn with the velocity as close to planned as possible.
