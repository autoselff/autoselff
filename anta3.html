A cozy game about building houses for penguins on ANTA3 Island, help the island grow and work off the debt you took to buy a house.

It's a bit cold here...

Sent to a cold, abandoned island, you meet its last inhabitant, who explains the mystery that drove the penguins away from their former home. You're tasked with building homes for the penguins so they'll return and restore the island to its former glory. This also gives you one last chance to repay the debt you incurred years earlier to buy the house.

BUILD HOUSES FOR LOCAL PENGUINS

Many different penguins will visit the island, each deciding to give their former home a second chance. They’ll ask you to build houses that finally suit them. With a building system based on freely rotatable blocks, you can create anything you want so long as it meets their requirements, such as minimum space and materials used. But don’t worry, you can also build anything outside the construction area for the good of the whole island.

BUILD RELATIONSHIPS WITH PENGUINS

Despite all the work, you still need time to relax. When there are no more orders at the moment, there's nothing stopping you from expanding and decorating the island, catching fish to gift or sell, or talking and bonding with the penguins who live there.

PAY OFF THE DEBT

After the job is completed, the penguins will decide whether they like the building or not. Much will depend on their decision, so it's worth the effort. If they like it, they'll pay you based on the final rating and move into the house. However, if they don't like the house, they might not even pay, which could lead to bankruptcy...
