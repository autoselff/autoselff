About

Hello! I'm AUTOSELFF, a 20-year-old computer science student from Poland. I mostly build software related to my current interests, but from time to time I also finish a video game. Over the past few years I've worked on several interesting projects, including an ecosystem simulation, a falling sand simulation, and my own amateur game framework. I've also released games such as HyperDissonance, Heart Keeper, and CatUp. I recently released a demo of ANTA3 on Steam and continue to work on the game. I'm also working on a few larger web-focused projects and have started a new game project on the side.
